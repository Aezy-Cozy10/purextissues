About Purex Tissues

Softness, delivered honestly.

Purex Tissues was founded on a simple idea: everyday hygiene products should feel premium without costing a fortune. From our facilities we manufacture and distribute tissue boxes, toilet rolls, kitchen rolls, hand sanitizers and cleaning products to households, offices and businesses across Pakistan.

Uncompromising Quality

Every batch is tested for softness, strength and absorbency before it reaches you.

Fast, Nationwide Delivery

Customer First
